Repositories must be added from the Telegram group or topic that will receive notifications.

How to add one:

1. Go to the target Telegram group or topic.
2. Open the group's Nuecagram menu and tap Management.
3. Tap + Add.

You can also run /setup in that group or topic.

No chat ID typing needed -- Nuecagram fills the destination automatically.
